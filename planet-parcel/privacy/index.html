Planet Parcel – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Planet Parcel ("the game") is published by Kezual Games ("we"). This policy explains what the game does with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your progress (stamps, upgrades, ship designs, courier rank, postmark album, best times) and your settings (sound, aim guide) are saved only on your device, in the game's own storage. We never receive this data. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Permissions and network

The game does not ask for any permissions such as location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos. It works offline.

Sharing

The "copy to share" button on the daily route copies a short text (your route result) to your clipboard so you can paste it where you like. Nothing is sent anywhere by the game itself.

Children

The game is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data, such as advertising or online leaderboards, we will update this policy and the date above before the feature is released.
