Planet Parcel – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Planet Parcel ("the game") is published by Kezual Games ("we"). This policy explains what the game does with your information.

What we collect

The game does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, or advertising ID.

To understand how the game is played and to fix problems, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service provided by Google. It records anonymous gameplay events, such as starting a run, how far a run got and how it ended, buying an upgrade, and finishing the daily route, together with basic technical information Firebase collects automatically (device model, operating system version, app version, country derived from your IP address, and session length). These events are linked to a random app-instance identifier, not to you.

We use this information only to improve the game. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

Google Analytics for Firebase

Firebase processes this data on our behalf under Google's terms. Advertising ID collection and ad personalisation are switched off. Google keeps event data for a limited time (we use the shortest retention setting available). You can read how Google handles this data at policies.google.com/privacy and firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

What stays on your device

Your progress (stamps, upgrades, ship designs, courier rank, postmark album, best times) and your settings (sound, vibration, aim guide) are saved only on your device, in the game's own storage. We never receive this data. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Permissions and network

The game does not ask for any permissions such as location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos. It works offline; analytics events are sent when a connection is available.

Sharing

The "copy to share" button on the daily route copies a short text (your route result) to your clipboard so you can paste it where you like.

Your choices

You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the game. To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your device, email us at the address below.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data, such as advertising or online leaderboards, we will update this policy and the date above before the feature is released.
