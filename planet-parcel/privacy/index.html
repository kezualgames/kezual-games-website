Planet Parcel – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Planet Parcel ("the game") is published by Kezual Games ("we"). This policy explains what the game does with your information.

What we collect

The game does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, contacts or precise location.

To understand how the game is played and to fix problems, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service provided by Google. It records gameplay events, such as starting a run, how far a run got and how it ended, buying an upgrade, finishing a story level and watching an ad, together with basic technical information Firebase collects automatically (device model, operating system version, app version, country and an app-instance identifier that is not linked to your name).

We use this information to improve the game and to measure how well its ads work. We do not sell it.

Ads

The game offers optional rewarded ads, shown only when you choose to watch one (for example to continue after a crash or to double the stamps from a run). There are no banners and no forced ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To load and show ads, and to measure them, AdMob and its partners may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and how you interact with ads, which can include showing you personalised ads. This can happen when the game starts, before you choose to watch an ad, because the ad is loaded in advance.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where it is required, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before loading ads. You can change your choice at any time under Settings, "Ad privacy". You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings, Google, Ads). Read how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Google Analytics for Firebase

Firebase processes this data on our behalf under Google's terms. We turn off Firebase's own use of the advertising ID and of ad personalisation signals (the ad provider described above still uses the advertising ID for ads). Google keeps event data for a limited time (we use up to 14 months). We may also keep anonymous event data in our own analysis storage so we can compare releases over time. You can read how Google handles this data at policies.google.com/privacy and firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

What stays on your device

Your progress (stamps, upgrades, ship designs, courier rank, postmark album, best times) and your settings (sound, vibration, aim guide) are saved only on your device, in the game's own storage. We never receive this data. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it. Android's own backup may copy it to your Google account if you have backups turned on.

Permissions and network

The game does not ask for permissions such as location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos. It uses the advertising ID permission so ads can work. The game plays offline; analytics events are sent, and ads need a connection.

Your choices

You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the game. You can decline or change ad consent under Settings, "Ad privacy" (where it is shown), reset your advertising ID in Android settings, or simply never watch an ad. To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your device, email us at the address below.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If a future version changes how the game handles data, for example by adding in-app purchases or online leaderboards, we will update this policy and the date above before the change is released.
